Exercise One · 36 minutes · hands on

Make something

Everyone asks Claude for one thing on the same topic. What comes back depends on how you asked, and on which shape you were given.

The topic

How context windows work: why long AI conversations can go wrong, and what to do about it.

Model settings for this exercise Claude Enterprise · Opus 5 · effort: Low

Low is a speed setting: it keeps every artifact arriving within a few minutes, so the whole group has something to show.

Count off

Groups of six: three in a row and the three behind them. Count off 1, 2, 3, 1, 2, 3, so every shape appears twice in your group. Your number is your shape.

  • 1a document
  • 2a slide deck (.pptx)
  • 3an interactive web page

The document people will be done first. The deck people will wait longer. That difference is information about what you asked for.

The floor prompt

This is the floor. You can do better. Put your shape in the blank.

Floor prompt 
I want to learn about the following topic:

How context windows work: why long AI conversations
can go wrong, and what to do about it.

Can you produce a ______ to teach me about this?

    1 = a document
    2 = a slide deck (.pptx)
    3 = an interactive web page

Write your brief before you send. Nobody sends until go.

After you send

Do not open your artifact yet. Pass your phone or laptop along the row and read each other's briefs. What did the best brief in your group include that yours did not?

Show and share

Two minutes each. Show your artifact to the group. The group asks one thing:

What did this shape make easy, and what did it hide?

The two people with the same number compare their two.

After the exercise: score your own artifact

Wait for the instructor. Then tick what your artifact does. Ticks stay in this browser only.
