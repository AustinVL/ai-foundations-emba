    Get set

    Model settings for this exercise Claude Enterprise · Opus 5 · effort: Medium

    If something does not work, ask someone next to you first, then raise your hand.

    The two files

    1. The CFO’s memo · a proposed 12% price rise on the Mid tier, with Finance’s numbers and the modeling rules. Opens in a new tab. One page. Download the file to your laptop; you attach it in Round B.
    2. The VP of Sales’s email · the reply. Opens in a new tab. Half a page. Download the file.

    File 1 · the CFO’s memo

    MEMO To: Executive leadership team From: Dana Whitfield, Chief Financial Officer Date: October 19, 2026 Re: Proposed 12% price increase, Mid tier SUMMARY We propose raising the Mid-tier price from $180 to $201.60 per month, effective at the start of next quarter. Finance projects that the increase raises revenue over the next 12 months, even after the extra churn and downgrades we expect. Details and assumptions are below. CURRENT BOOK OF BUSINESS (as of October 1, 2026) Entry: 1,260 accounts at $65/month (MRR $81,900), monthly churn 3.8% Mid: 2,604 accounts at $180/month (MRR $468,720), monthly churn 2.1% Enterprise: 336 accounts at $940/month (MRR $315,840), monthly churn 0.7% Total: 4,200 accounts, MRR $866,460 FINANCE’S ESTIMATE OF THE IMPACT - Mid-tier churn rises from 2.1% to 2.6% per month. Basis: the response to our 2024 Entry-tier increase, and two industry pricing studies. - 5% of Mid-tier accounts move down to Entry, once, when the new price takes effect. PLANNING CONVENTIONS (please use these in any model of this decision) - 12-month horizon, starting the month the new price takes effect. - Existing accounts only. No new customer acquisition in either scenario. - The downgrade happens once, at the change. Downgraded accounts pay the Entry price and churn at the Entry rate from then on. - Entry and Enterprise prices and churn rates are unchanged. - The comparison is against keeping the current price: Mid churn stays at 2.1% and nobody downgrades. RECOMMENDATION Proceed with the increase.

    File 2 · the VP of Sales’s email

    From: Jordan Okafor, VP of Sales To: Dana Whitfield; Executive leadership team Date: October 21, 2026 Subject: RE: Proposed 12% price increase, Mid tier Dana, Thanks for circulating this. I don’t disagree with the logic of a price increase, but I think Finance’s numbers are too optimistic. My team has spoken with about 40 of our larger Mid-tier accounts over the last two weeks. A lot of them are already under budget pressure, and a 12% increase is going to land badly. Based on those conversations, I’d plan for: - Mid-tier churn going to 3.4% a month, not 2.6%. - 15% of Mid-tier accounts dropping to Entry when the new price takes effect, not 5%. Everything else in your memo matches what we see. But if my numbers are closer to right, I’m not convinced this increase pays for itself. Jordan

    Attached are a memo from our CFO and an email from our VP of Sales about a proposed 12% price rise on our mid tier. Build an interactive web page I can use in a leadership meeting: a twelve-month projection of monthly recurring revenue with and without the price rise, with sliders for the most uncertain assumptions. Show clearly when the price rise pays off.

    This is the floor. You can do better: who is the page for, what do they already believe, what will they argue about? Write your brief and send it. Nobody waits.
