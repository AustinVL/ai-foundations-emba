Memo

To: Executive leadership team

From: Dana Whitfield, Chief Financial Officer

Date: October 19, 2026

Re: Proposed 12% price increase, Mid tier

Summary

We propose raising the Mid-tier price from $180 to $201.60 per month, effective at the start of next quarter. Finance projects that the increase raises revenue over the next 12 months, even after the extra churn and downgrades we expect. Details and assumptions are below.

Finance’s estimate of the impact

  • Mid-tier churn rises from 2.1% to 2.6% per month. Basis: the response to our 2024 Entry-tier increase, and two industry pricing studies.
  • 5% of Mid-tier accounts move down to Entry, once, when the new price takes effect.

Planning conventions (please use these in any model of this decision)

  • 12-month horizon, starting the month the new price takes effect.
  • Existing accounts only. No new customer acquisition in either scenario.
  • The downgrade happens once, at the change. Downgraded accounts pay the Entry price and churn at the Entry rate from then on.
  • Entry and Enterprise prices and churn rates are unchanged.
  • The comparison is against keeping the current price: Mid churn stays at 2.1% and nobody downgrades.

Recommendation

Proceed with the increase.
