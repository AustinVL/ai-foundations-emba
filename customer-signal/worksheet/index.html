Two immediate tests

Run two tests inside the app, against the analysis it already shows. One should check grounding: does the evidence in the app support what the app says? One should challenge a conclusion.

  1. Trace five displayed quotations to their source reviews in the evidence panel. Judge whether the surrounding text supports the assigned theme.
  2. Take one reported count or frequency. Reconstruct it from the Details tab or the review inspector.
  3. Pick one proposed priority and look for evidence against it.
  4. Check whether the app separates a frequent inconvenience from a rarer severe failure.
  5. Compare one conclusion with a simple baseline: star ratings, or a human read of a random handful of reviews.
  6. Find a roadmap recommendation that goes beyond what reviews alone can establish.
