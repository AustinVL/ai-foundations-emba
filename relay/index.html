Your task

Read your role card. Record up to three one-line notes. When the clock ends, the card is gone.

Your executive team then writes one prompt that produces a clear decision, a persuasive 200–250-word pitch the CEO can deliver to the board, and a decision-and-board-ask statement of 60 words or fewer.

One prompt, one response.

What you need

  • Your own phone or laptop, to read your role card and keep your notes.
  • One laptop at the table signed in to Claude Enterprise, to write the prompt and run it once.
