What is in it

28 text files, four original PDFs and two images, 17.6 MB zipped. Every text file starts with a header naming the original and how it was converted. Check anything consequential (a time, a room, a date, a dollar amount, a deadline) against the original or SloanHub before relying on it.

Orientation weekend

  • emba28-orientation-agenda.md: the Sept 18 to 19 agenda, every session, time, room and speaker. Original kept as EMBA 28 Orientation Agenda (Sep 14 2026).pdf.
  • emba28-orientation-welcome-slides.md: the Friday welcome deck: programs, mission, team, class facts, backpack, checklist.
  • emba28-orientation-program-overview-slides.md: the academic overview and support services deck: curriculum, LIM week, norms, values, resources. Its two schedule images are in images/.
  • emba28-orientation-mit-leadership-center-session-slides.md: the MIT Leadership Center session deck: psychological safety, emotions, mindfulness.

People

  • emba28-bio-book.md: all 132 classmates as text: name, pronunciation, title and employer, location, bio. The original EMBA 2028 Bio Book.pdf is kept for the photos.
  • emba28-advisor-list.md: which program advisor each student is assigned to, with MIT email addresses.
  • emba28-staff-sheet.md: the Executive Degree Programs staff, 17 people with titles and emails. Original kept as Staff Sheet 2028.pdf.

Dates, money, logistics

  • emba28-program-calendar.md: every class date from Sept 2026 to May 2028 by type. Original kept as EMBA28 Program Calendar.pdf.
  • emba28-tuition-and-registration-chart.md: payments and due dates, term dates, subjects and units per term.
  • emba-hotel-reservation-links.md: Marriott room blocks for each class weekend, with deadlines, rates and booking links; the Royal Sonesta codes.
  • 2026-09-08-announcements-email.md and 2026-09-15-announcements-email.md: the two most recent weekly announcement emails.
  • required-atlas-training-modules.md: the two required online training modules.
  • x360-assessment-and-executive-leadership-coaching.md: the x360 assessment and coaching opt-in, with its timeline.
  • academic-integrity-top-6-policy-violations.md: the six most common academic integrity violations.

Advice and pre-reading

  • fall-2026-emba-survival-guide.md: the Class of 2027's guide for the Class of 2028.
  • emba28-day-1-ai-foundations-handout.md: Prompting Is Briefing, the pre-reading for this session.
  • 2026-06-17-welcome-academics-student-experience-webinar.md: the June 17 admitted-student webinar deck.

Public pages and the rest

  • public/: the ten public MIT Sloan EMBA web pages as text, each with its source address.
  • README.txt: this list, with the conversion notes.

What was left out

The other cohorts' bio books (personal data, and not about this program). Photos, decorative slides and page furniture are not carried into the text. Click-tracking links in the emails were dropped; direct links and office email addresses were kept.
