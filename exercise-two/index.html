Build your assistant

A claude.ai Project on your own EMBA orientation materials. You work on your own. Your group of six stays together for the share-out.

  • Your EMBA orientation materials as files on your laptop: the program calendar, the handbook or policies, the pre-reading list, anything you were sent for orientation.
  • The worksheet, open in another tab.

The task

You will paste this twice: once in a bare chat, once inside your Project.

The task 
Help me prepare for the next two class weekends. Identify required preparation, dates and logistics I need to act on, and the appropriate contacts for unresolved questions. Distinguish program requirements from your recommendations, and identify the source for each program-specific claim.

The Project instructions

Paste this into the Project's instructions, then edit one line to fit you.

Project instructions template 
You help a first-year MIT Sloan Executive MBA participant prepare for the program. Answer from the uploaded materials. For every program-specific claim, name the document and the section it comes from. If the materials do not cover something, say so and suggest who to ask. Keep program requirements separate from your own recommendations.

The steps

MinuteStepWhat to do
0 to 2 Get in Open claude.ai. Projects is in the left rail. Your materials are files on your laptop. The worksheet is open.
2 to 6 Baseline

A fresh, ordinary chat, not in a Project. Paste the task. Read what it does.

On the worksheet, tick what happened, and underline every specific claim it made. If it asks for your documents, that is the right behavior: a model that does not have the materials should ask for them.
6 to 14 Build

Give it what it just asked for. New Project, named "EMBA assistant". Upload your materials. Paste the instructions template and edit one line to fit you.
14 to 19 Ask The same task, inside the Project, plus one constraint of your own: travel, a work deadline, family.
19 to 27 Verify

Pick two consequential claims: a date, a requirement. Find each in your documents: match, partly, or not found.

Then ask one question the materials cannot answer, such as parking on class weekends, or a policy you know is not in there. Does it say so, or improvise?
27 to 33 Improve

Change the instructions or add a document because of what you found. Then a new chat in the Project, with the second task below.

If you build these systems for a living: find an unsupported inference, an ambiguous instruction, or a conflict between two sources, and fix how the assistant handles it.
33 to 38 Share-out Two or three voices from the room. The three questions are below.
38 to 40 Write it down On the worksheet: one use case, one test, one decision.
The second task, for the Improve step 
Draft a note to my manager about the time commitment for the next two months, with dates and what I need from them.

Share-out

  1. What did the bare chat do?
  2. What did the Project get right that the bare chat could not?
  3. What would you still not trust it on, and how would you find out?

If something does not work

  • No Projects on your account. Paste the materials into one chat and continue from the Ask step.
  • An upload fails on the Wi-Fi. Use the starter pack from this site. It is small.
  • No materials at all. Use the starter pack.
