# MIT EMBA Program Schedule

The MIT EMBA program’s 20-month curriculum is designed for working executives, with classes typically convening every three weeks. The program includes 26 weekend sessions, four week-long modules for integrated learning, and an additional week of team projects with the option of an immersive international trip.
