## What defines the MIT Executive MBA experience?

The MIT Executive MBA (EMBA) is an MBA program for mid-career professionals that includes a cohort-based experience, data-driven curriculum, and flexible scheduling designed for working leaders.

The MIT EMBA Experience includes:

1. **A cohort of experienced professionals**
   MIT EMBA students learn alongside senior leaders from diverse industries and global backgrounds, creating a collaborative, high-impact learning environment. [Meet some of our alumni.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/emba/student-profiles)
2. **A** r**igorous, analytics-focused curriculum**
   The MIT Executive MBA curriculum teaches the science of management, combining strategy, data analysis, and leadership to support better decision-making. [Explore the curriculum.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/emba/program-curriculum)
3. **Applied learning through Action Learning**
   MIT EMBA students complete hands-on projects that apply classroom concepts to real business challenges, often within their own organizations, in line with MIT's motto *mens et manus* (mind and hand).  [Gain an understanding of Action Learning.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/emba/action-learning)
4. **A program format tailored for an executive schedule**
   The MIT Executive MBA schedule includes classes on campus every two to three weekends\* and immersive week-long modules, allowing students to continue working full-time. [View the program schedule.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/emba/program-schedule)
5. **Access to MIT Sloan faculty expertise**
   Students learn from MIT Sloan faculty who are leaders in management, finance, technology, and innovation. [Meet our faculty.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/emba/the-mit-impact#faculty-profiles)
6. **A global MIT alumni network**
   Graduates of the MIT Executive MBA join a worldwide network of more than 148,000 MIT alumni and a strong MIT Sloan and EMBA community. [Experience our community.](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/alumni#community)

\*Up to three weekends may transition to fully-remote as part of our pilot flexibility program.
